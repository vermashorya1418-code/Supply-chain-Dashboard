# Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyses inventory, product sales, transportation, shipping costs, delivery time, and product quality to help understand supply chain performance and identify areas for operational improvement.

## Objectives
- Monitor key supply chain performance indicators.
- Analyse inventory levels and product sales.
- Compare shipping costs and delivery duration across routes and transportation modes.
- Examine lead times and defect rates.
- Support data-driven decisions related to logistics and inventory management.

## Tools Used
- **Microsoft Power BI** – dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures
- **Microsoft Excel** – source dataset

## Dataset
The dashboard uses a supply chain dataset containing product, inventory, transportation, shipping, cost, sales, and quality-related information.

Key fields include:
- SKU and product information
- Route and transportation mode
- Shipping carrier, shipping time, and shipping cost
- Lead time and defect rate
- Stock levels and quantity sold
- Revenue generated and product costs
- Location

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard includes the following KPI cards:

- Average Cost
- Average Defect Rate
- Average Shipping Duration
- Average Stock Level
- Total Cost
- Total Locations
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Sales / Revenue
- Total Shipping Costs
- Total SKUs

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI Overview:** A summary of important supply chain metrics.
- **Route Analysis:** Comparison of recorded routes based on shipping duration, shipping costs, and other operational measures.
- **Transportation Mode Analysis:** Evaluation of air, rail, road, and sea transportation using available cost and time metrics.
- **Inventory Analysis:** Review of stock levels and quantity sold.
- **Shipping and Lead-Time Analysis:** Exploration of delivery duration and lead-time patterns.
- **Product Quality Analysis:** Examination of defect rates across available categories.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Users can explore the data through available slicers and visual filters.

## Business Insights and Applications
The dashboard helps users identify patterns in shipping costs and delivery times, compare the performance of recorded routes and transportation modes, and review inventory and defect-rate indicators. These findings can inform logistics planning, stock management, and further operational investigation.

**Note:** The dataset contains route categories and recorded shipping metrics, but does not include detailed origin-destination coordinates, distances, or vehicle capacity. Therefore, the dashboard compares recorded route performance; it does not calculate the physically shortest or mathematically optimal transportation route. The analysis is descriptive and does not establish the causes of observed differences.

## Conclusion
The Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard provides a consolidated view of key logistics, inventory, cost, and quality measures. By making these indicators easier to explore, it supports data-informed assessment of supply chain performance and highlights areas for further investigation and improvement.
